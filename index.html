A repeatable method for recovering family histories from online archives through AI-assisted record linkage. Developed on a Holocaust case, and not limited to one.

The Case One prisoner card became more than three hundred records, two cousins Helen had described but never named, and a living branch of the family whose tree was missing two of Iczik’s sons. Shown in the order it was worked. The Method Seven steps you can follow on your own family, each one with something concrete to go and do. No training and no subscription required. Databases Every collection used here, with links, what each one holds, what it costs, and the one trick that makes it work. Most are free.

Where it started

The family had one prisoner card and assumed that was the end of it. The research began in 2026, and it is now a dossier of more than three hundred records. None of it required travel. Read how it was found.

The standard

An identification is admitted only where independent record types corroborate one another without contradiction, and every linkage is verified against the primary document. Where the evidence supports a probable conclusion rather than a proven one, it is stated as probable.
