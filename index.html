The journey

Helen, in brief

Helen Lando Helman was born in Tiszabogdány, a village in the Carpathians, in 1929. She was the only one of her immediate family to survive the war. Her mother, her father and five of her brothers and sisters were murdered in 1944. For decades there was nothing of them: no photograph, no grave, no dates.

In 2019 the Arolsen Archives put their records online, and my mother found one prisoner card: Helen’s father, Zacharias. The family assumed that was the end of it. The research began in 2026, and it has grown to more than three hundred records. None of it required travel. Follow her road on the map, or see the hints and mistakes it taught me.

The method, drawn the way an engineer would

One card in. A family out.

I’m a process engineer, so this is how I see it. One record goes in. It passes through four steps. What comes out verified goes straight back in as the next search. What doesn’t hold up is set aside, and I keep a list of those too. That loop ran hundreds of times. Tap any box for real examples from Helen’s case.

Each box links to its examples on the method page. “Set aside” links to my mistakes.

Who I am, and why

Why I do this

I do this research from Hawai‘i, about as far from Europe as you can get.

I speak only English. The records I work with are in Hungarian, Czech, German, Russian, Yiddish and Hebrew, so I use AI to help me read and translate them, and then I check what it tells me against the page. I was never trained in this. I’m a process engineer, and I’m getting my master’s in chemical engineering at Stanford. Engineers take a finished thing apart to see how it was made. I do the same with a family: I reverse-engineer what happened from the records the system left behind. That’s why this is called Engineering Remembrance. I’m not a historian. If I can learn it, you can too.

My grandparents on my mother’s side both survived the Holocaust: Helen Lando Helman and Tibi Katz. When I was born, Helen retired and lived with us part of every week to help raise me. I was the youngest, and she slept in my bed with me.

This site uses her family as a case study. Everything here was found online, from home, and every step can be repeated for another family.
