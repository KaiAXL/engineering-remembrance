Sources and credits

Helen’s Road was made by Samantha Seligman-Grajewski.

Helen’s voice

Every word Helen speaks here is cut from her own recordings: tapes made in 2008, and a talk she gave in the early 2000s. The words on screen are transcripts of those clips.

My voice and the music

Every voice here is a real recording: my own, Samantha Seligman-Grajewski, and Helen’s. Music: Antonín Dvořák, “Goin’ Home,” performed by the United States Air Force Band (public domain).
